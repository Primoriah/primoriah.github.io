ABOUT PRIMORIAH

A study of ideas expressed through what we create.

The Beginning

Primoriah began with a simple question: what if clothing could carry something more than an image?

Not simply a trend, a statement, or decoration, but an idea worth returning to.

What We Create

Primoriah creates wearable works built around symbolism, philosophy, psychology, and human experience.

Each work begins with an idea and develops into something that can be worn, questioned, and interpreted.

An Ongoing Study

Primoriah is not built around having all the answers.

It is an ongoing study of meaning, creativity, symbolism, and the ways ideas influence the people who encounter them.
