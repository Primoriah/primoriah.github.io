Overview

The Fool represents the beginning of a journey: stepping forward without certainty, accepting the unknown, and allowing experience to shape what comes next.

The Story

Every journey begins with a step into the unknown. The Fool exists as a reflection on beginnings, uncertainty, and the courage required to move forward.

Symbolism
